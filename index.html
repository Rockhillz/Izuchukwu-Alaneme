About

I'm a backend engineer with a little over a year of production experience, all of it spent inside fintech. At Monirates I work across two systems: a cross-border FX payment platform and a custodial multi-chain wallet backend — which means most of my days involve ledgers, reconciliation workflows, retries, and making sure a partner's money lands where it's supposed to, even when the network in between doesn't cooperate.

Before that I trained through Gomycode's full-stack programme, and before that I spent over a year as a data entry officer — which, oddly, is where I first learned to care about data integrity and audit trails, long before I knew the word "reconciliation."

Outside of feature work, I maintain nodecore-kit, an open-source Node.js toolkit I built to stop rewriting the same Redis clients and AWS helpers for every new service, and I act as the technical point of contact for external developers integrating our APIs.
